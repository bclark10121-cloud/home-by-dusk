# Home by Dusk v0.4.0

The whip and a livelier Wren. Wren's main weapon is now a leather whip that the game draws live on every screen frame, so it bends, rolls out and cracks at the tip. The rusty sword moves to the second weapon button. Wren's run has twice as many frames, bigger arm and leg swings and a bounce. She leans into her speed, eases in and out of a run and kicks up dust.

This is stage 1 of the motion plan you approved on 2026-11-15.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK over the last one. The title card shows Version 0.4.0.
2. Start or continue. Look at the two weapon slots: the Leather Whip is first and the Rusty Sword is second.
3. Walk to the training dummy. Stop about three steps away from it.
4. Press the first weapon button once (J, X or the touch button). Wren lifts the whip back over her shoulder. It rolls out in a curve and cracks at the tip with a small ring and a snap sound. The dummy takes the hit from well outside sword reach.
5. Hold the same button. The whip keeps cracking, about twice a second.
6. Press the second weapon button three times (K, Y or the touch button). The sword combo is the same as in v0.3.0.
7. Run right across the open ground. Watch her legs and arms: every screen frame shows a new pose. She leans forward and small dust puffs rise at her feet.
8. Let go of the stick. She eases to a stop in place of stopping dead.
9. Run and flick the other way. She pinches for a blink, kicks up dust and turns.
10. Run, then crack the whip without stopping. She slows to a walk for the strike and her steps slow with her.

## New in this version

- Leather Whip, Wren's own weapon. It reaches about 4 tiles, more than twice the sword's reach. It hits once for 4, at the moment of the crack. One press is one crack. Hold the button and it cracks again and again.
- The whip is drawn live. It is a curve of 26 short pieces that the game bends on every screen frame: it trails behind her in the wind-up, a loop travels from the handle to the tip in the strike and the tip snaps straight. It then droops and reels back into her hand. It is not a picture in her hand, as the other weapons are.
- A new whip pose for Wren with 8 frames: reach back, throw the arm over, hold and recover.
- The run went from 8 frames at 12 a second to 16 frames at 30 a second. Arm and leg swings are about a third bigger. The body rises and falls with each step and tilts forward.
- Her steps follow her real speed. A slow start or a strike on the move plays the run slower, so her feet slide less.
- Lean. She tilts into her speed on the ground and a little in the air. The tilt eases in and out.
- A softer start and stop. She reaches full speed in about a tenth of a second and stops in a little less. Top speed is the same.
- Dust at each footfall, a bigger puff and a quick pinch when she turns at speed.
- Two new sounds: a rush of air for the whip and a sharp crack.

## Changed

- A new trip starts with the whip and the rusty sword. Heading home sets Wren's gear back to these two.
- A trip saved in an older version continues with the whip in the first slot. The weapon that was there moves to the second slot. If the first slot held the rusty sword and the second held another weapon, that other weapon stays.
- The whip and the rusty sword never drop from foes and never show on Pip's cart.
- A strike now repeats only while you hold the button or press again during it. In v0.3.0 one quick press near the end of a swing sometimes started a second swing.
- The sign at the training dummy now names both weapon buttons.
- The Vine Whip, which waits in the full gear list, uses the same live whip in green.

## Not changed

- Jump height, double jump, run speed and roll distance. A bot measured them again: 3.14 tiles, 5.30 tiles, 8.8 tiles a second and 3.8 tiles.
- The level, the foes, the music, the saves and the controls.
- The sword, hammer, bow and the other weapons.

## Full test checklist

1. The whip: wind-up, roll out, crack and reel in, facing left and right.
2. The whip's reach. Is 4 tiles right, too long or too short?
3. The whip's speed. One crack takes half a second. Is the wind-up too slow?
4. Hold to keep cracking. Does it feel right, or should it need a press each time?
5. Whip a mossling, a puffcap and a moth. A moth in the air is the hard one: the whip strikes level.
6. Crack the whip while running and while in the air.
7. The run at full speed: smoother or not? Say what still looks stiff.
8. The lean. Is it too much, too little or right?
9. Starts and stops. Does Wren feel slippery now? The Feel tools in Tester tools change this live.
10. The dust. Too much, too little or right?
11. Continue an old save. The whip is in slot one and your old weapon is in slot two.
12. The sword combo, hammer, bow and abilities work as before.
13. Sound: the rush and the crack. Tell me if the crack is too sharp or too quiet.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried. The 3D test page is there to get the first real phone number.
- How the whip and the run feel in your hands. I judged them from a 60 frames a second recording of a scripted run, frame by frame. A recording shows motion. It does not show feel.
- Nobody listened to the two new sounds. The sound test confirms sound comes out of the page.
- Sound on Android.
- The browser tests ran with a 640 x 360 page, as before.
