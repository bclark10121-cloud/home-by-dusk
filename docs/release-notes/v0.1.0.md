# Home by Dusk v0.1.0

The first beta. One small level, the Dusk Hollow, to tune how the game feels. Wren is the hero made through the Gemini and Meshy workflow, rendered into pixel art.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK. The title card shows Home by Dusk, Version 0.1.0 and the controls for the device you used last. Press Start.
2. Walk right past the cottage. Wren is the hooded traveller with the amber scarf. Signs explain each move as you reach them, in the words of your device.
3. Swing at the training dummy with J (keyboard), X (Xbox) or the first weapon button (touch). Three quick presses make a three hit combo. Numbers show each hit.
4. Jump the steps and climb the lookout: walk into a low ledge and Wren climbs it. Jump at a higher one and she grabs the edge. Press jump again in the air for a double jump.
5. Roll (L, B or Roll) through a mossling as it lunges. Nothing touches you mid roll.
6. Find the throwing knives on top of the tall wall and the hunting bow above the wooden ledges. Use them with U (LT) and K (Y).
7. Break the old cracked wall with three hits. A secret message shows and a chest waits inside with coins and an item.
8. Walk to Pip's cart at the end. Press E (RB) to talk. Buy something, or a cup of tea for energy.
9. Go through the glowing door. Run 2 starts with your gear. The items in the hollow change and Pip has new stock.
10. Open the Menu (Esc, View or Menu). Try Journal, then Head home: the first press asks, the second sends Wren home. Coins and keepsakes stay. The gear goes back to the rusty sword.

## New in this version

- The Dusk Hollow: one small level with a cottage, a training dummy, a hollow pit, a tall wall, wooden ledges, an old cracked wall with a secret, a high tower, Pip's cart and the door.
- Movement: run, variable jump, double jump, coyote time, jump buffer, roll with dodge frames, auto ledge climb and drop through wooden ledges.
- Gear: two weapon slots and two ability slots, each on its own button. 20 weapons in 7 types and 20 abilities in 10 types.
- Gear sources: lying in the hollow, dropped by foes, the secret chest and Pip's cart. Full hands ask which slot to swap.
- Coins from foes and chests. Energy instead of lives. Runs through the door, trips home.
- Three foes that warn before they strike: mossling, puffcap and moth. Burn, slow, freeze, stun and root.
- Six keepsakes, one secret, a journal and records.
- Hero sprites rendered from the Meshy model: run from Meshy's run clip, every other move posed on its skeleton.
- Five save slots, Settings with Camera, Foe hits (Normal, Gentle, None), Damage numbers and Ledge climb. Tester tools.
- Music: "Grasslands" (CC0). Sound effects made for this game.

## Full test checklist

1. Title card: Start, Play and Settings work with touch, keyboard and an Xbox controller.
2. Settings: every switch changes at once and stays after a restart. Foe hits None means no energy loss.
3. Save slots: a new slot starts a new trip. Delete needs two presses.
4. Movement: jump, double jump, roll, ledge climb, drop through a wooden ledge.
5. Every weapon type and ability type, through Tester tools, Gear.
6. A foe hit costs energy. At zero energy Wren heads home. Coins and keepsakes stay.
7. Swap dialog: pick up an item with both slots full. The old item drops.
8. Pip's cart: buy an item, buy tea, leave with B or Esc.
9. The door starts run 2 with your gear.
10. Keepsakes and the secret show in the journal and stay after a reload.
11. Touch: walk, jump, roll, both weapons, both abilities and Use.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried. The browser test used the kit's pretend controller.
- Sound was not listened to.
- Touch controls were not tried on a small phone.
