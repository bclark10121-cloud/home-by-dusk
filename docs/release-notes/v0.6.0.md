# Home by Dusk v0.6.0

Wren moves between moves. She pushes off into a run, leans back to brake, swings round to face you when she turns at speed, tucks at the top of a jump, reaches down as she falls and lands with a longer bend. Her idle, jumps, landing and hit come from real animation clips (KayKit, free to use) copied onto her own skeleton. A second test link draws Wren live in 3D inside the real Dusk Hollow, with a frame counter, so you can judge the look and the speed on your phone.

This is stage 3A of the motion plan, with the live 3D test you chose on 2026-11-16.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK over the last one. The title card shows Version 0.6.0.
2. Start or continue. Stand still and watch Wren. She breathes and shifts her weight.
3. Press right once from standing. She leans and pushes off into the run.
4. Let go at full speed. She leans back and brakes for a moment, then stands.
5. Run right, then flick left at full speed. She swings round to face you for a blink, then turns into her new way and runs.
6. Jump and hold the button. She springs off, tucks her legs at the top, reaches her feet down as she falls and bends her knees when she lands. Jump off a high ledge for the deepest landing.
7. Bump into a foe (turn off Peaceful foes if it is on). She recoils from the hit.
8. On your phone, open the game page and tap "live 3D test in the hollow" on the Testers line. Walk and run for 20 seconds. Tap Quality and repeat for Medium and Low. Send me the three Result lines and say whether live Wren belongs in the painting.

## New in this version

- Run start: three frames as she pushes off, leaning into her first steps.
- Stop: four frames as she leans back and brakes when you let go at speed.
- Turn: three frames as she swings round through facing the camera when she turns at speed.
- Jump: four take-off frames, three frames at the top of the jump and three falling frames with her feet reaching down.
- Landing: five frames in place of two. A hard landing shows all of it. A soft one shows the start of it.
- Idle: eight frames in place of six, from a real breathing idle.
- Hit: three frames from a real recoil.
- Pick up: four frames from a real pick up (not used in play yet).
- A live 3D test in the real level: a separate browser build at its own link. While Wren walks or runs she is drawn live in 3D, her walk and run blended by speed. Every other move uses the game's pictures. It shows frames a second and gives a result line for High, Medium and Low quality.

## Fixed

- After a turn at speed, Wren flipped back to face her old way for about a tenth of a second. The turn pinch now eases toward her new way.
- The run was meant to have a small forward lean built in. The sign was wrong, so it leaned back by 4 degrees. It now leans forward.

## Not changed

- Jump height, run speed, roll distance and every other feel number. The new moves are pictures only.
- The roll and the ledge climb. The free clip pack has no roll or climb, so they keep their old poses.
- The whip, the sword and every strike. The level, the gear list, saves and settings. A trip saved in v0.5.0 continues in v0.6.0.

## What was tested

- In a headless game: the route to the door, every find reachable, the feel numbers (the same as v0.5.0), the whip checks and a new check that the start, stop, turn, take-off, top, fall and landing all play. All passed.
- In a browser with a pretend Xbox controller: strikes and music, menus, saves and the debug code pad, real sound output and a v0.5.0 trip continuing in v0.6.0. All passed.
- The live 3D build in a browser: it loads, starts a trip and shows the live model while Wren walks. The test browser has no graphics chip, so its speed says nothing about a phone.
- A 60 frames a second recording of the demo routine, judged frame by frame. The demo now ends with a run, a turn at speed and a stop.
- The APK is signed with your key and asks for no internet permission. The game and the APK carry no 3D model.

## Not tested

- Nobody has played the new moves. They were judged from a recording.
- The live 3D test on a real phone. That is your step 8.
- No real tablet, Retroid or Xbox controller. Sound on Android.

## Judgment calls

- The clips come from KayKit Character Animations by Kay Lousberg, free tier, CC0. Credit is not required. The game credits it anyway.
- The clips are copied onto Wren by the direction of each bone. Twist is not copied. Wren's short legs and long coat keep her own look.
- Start, stop and turn are mixed from the clips with a lean or a turn added, because the pack has no start, stop or turn clips.
- All frames still fit on one hero picture (1936 x 1728, under the 2048 limit), so no second sheet was needed.
- A landing shows only when Wren is slow or still. Landing at a run goes straight into the run.
- The live 3D test shows the live model only while Wren walks or runs, because her model has only walk and run clips. It uses the full 10,000 triangle model, the same as the phone test page.
- The live 3D picture is redrawn on every frame, even while her pictures show, so the count is the worst case.
- No Meshy work and no Gemini pictures this release.
