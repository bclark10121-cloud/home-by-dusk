# Home by Dusk v0.9.0

Play holding your phone upright or sideways. Back now sits at the top left of every menu screen, clear of Android's bottom bar.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.8.0. Your saves stay.
2. Hold the phone upright and open the game. The start screen fits the tall screen.
3. Tap Settings. Back sits at the top left. The list scrolls under it. Tap Back.
4. Tap Play, pick your slot and walk the hollow upright for a minute. Wren keeps her size and the dusk sky fills the space above.
5. Open the Menu. Upright, its buttons sit under the energy bar.
6. Open How to play, then the Journal. Each has Back at the top left.
7. Visit Pip's cart. Back sits at the top left.
8. Turn the phone sideways mid-trip. The trip keeps going and the view switches.

## New in this version

- Full play upright and sideways, on Android and in the browser. The game turns with your phone.
- Upright: the same zoom with the painted sky above the hollow, and the Menu row under the energy bar.
- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, How to play, the Journal, Pip's cart and the swap choice. Close at the top right is gone.
- Turning the phone mid-trip keeps your trip. Nothing reloads.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0: the shared header bar with Back at the top and the screen fit for both orientations.

## Install

Version 0.9.0 installs over 0.8.0 and keeps your saves. The game follows your phone's rotation lock. Turn the lock off to play upright.
