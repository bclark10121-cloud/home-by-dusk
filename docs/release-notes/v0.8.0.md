# Home by Dusk v0.8.0

Release 7: the standards review pieces every game now shares. A Records page with 12 achievements and your stats, button remapping, backup codes for your saves, Battery saver and music at one steady level.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.7.0, or open the game page and press Play in your browser. Your saves stay. The start screen shows Version 0.8.0.
2. Press Continue or Play and pick your slot. Walk, jump and fight for a minute.
3. Open the Menu. Press Records. You see 12 achievements, then your stats: keepsakes, secrets, coins, trips home, doors, foes and best combo. If you played before, anything you already did shows as Earned.
4. Find a keepsake or go through the door. A gold note slides in when an achievement is earned.
5. Open Settings, then Change controls. Pick Roll and press another key or button. If another action had it, the two swap. Reset controls puts everything back.
6. In Settings, turn on Battery saver and play for a moment. Turn it off again.
7. Open the slot list. Press Copy code on your slot. Paste it into the box under the slots on another device and press Load code.
8. Listen to the music on the start screen, in the hollow and at Pip's cart. It should sound about as loud as before.

## New in this version

- Records in the Menu: 12 achievements and 7 stats on one page (G4.10, G4.11). Each achievement comes from something the game already counts, so nothing new to grind.
- Change controls in Settings for walking, down, jump, roll, both weapons, both abilities, Use and the journal (G5.22).
- Battery saver in Settings (G6.12).
- Backup codes in the slot list: Copy code and Load code (G5.19).
- Old saves are now tested every release (G5.24).
- An error log on the device. Copy bug report in Tester tools adds the last ten errors (G5.27).
- Music brought to one loudness level (G5.28). The game turns it up by the same amount, so the mix stays the same.
- Back sits at the top left of Settings, the slot list and Records (G5.30). The journal, How to play and Pip's cart get it in the next standards release.
