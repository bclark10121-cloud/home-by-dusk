# Home by Dusk v0.3.0

Sound in the browser, dark music and weapons with real motion. The browser builds of v0.1.0 and v0.2.0 were silent: no music and no sound effects. That is fixed. The game now has three dark, eerie music tracks from Patch.gg, to keep the spooky mood you asked for. Every strike has a wind-up, a fast swing and a follow-through, and the weapon glides through an arc and leaves a streak.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK over the last one. Turn your sound on. The title card shows Version 0.3.0.
2. Click or press any key on the title card. A quiet, eerie tune plays ("Whispers from beyond").
3. Press Start or Continue. The music fades to the hollow's tune ("Witch's lair").
4. Jump and land. You hear a jump and a landing.
5. Walk to the training dummy and press the first weapon button once (J, X or the touch button). Watch the sword: it lifts back over Wren's shoulder, sweeps down in an arc with a pale streak behind it, follows through low and comes back up.
6. Press three times for the combo: a chop, a rising backhand and a heavy two-handed finisher. Each hit holds the game still for a blink. The finisher holds a little longer.
7. Walk while you swing. Wren moves a little faster during a strike than before.
8. Pick up the throwing knives on the tall wall and use them (U, LT or the first ability button). Wren makes a throwing motion now.
9. Pick up the hunting bow above the plank ledges and shoot (K or Y). She raises the bow, draws, looses and lowers it.
10. Talk to Pip at the cart. The music fades to the cart's tune ("Catacombs"). Leave and the hollow's tune comes back.

## Fixed

- No sound in the browser. The game added its Music and Sound volume channels while it ran. In the browser, sounds sent to channels made that way stay silent. The channels now come from a file the game loads at the start. Music and every sound effect now play in the browser. A new test listens to the page's real sound output on every release.

## New in this version

- Dark music from Patch.gg, all from one maker's "Evil" pack and all CC0: "Whispers from beyond" on the title card, "Witch's lair" in the hollow and "Catacombs" at Pip's cart. Tracks fade into each other. "Grasslands" from the first two versions is gone: it was too bright for a spooky game.
- Strikes redone. The three sword strikes, the spear thrust, the hammer slam, the bow shot, the axe throw and the whip crack went from 2 or 3 frames each to between 4 and 9: a wind-up, a fast swing, a follow-through and a recovery. 52 strike frames in place of 22.
- The weapon glides. It moves on every tick between the body's frames. It trails behind the arm going in and whips ahead of it going out.
- A pale streak follows the blade through each swing.
- A landed hit holds the game still for a blink, longer for the hammer and the combo finisher.
- The weapon grows a touch at the moment it swings.
- Using an ability shows a motion: a throw for knives and bombs, a cast for the rest.
- Wren keeps a little more of her walking speed during a strike.

## Not changed

- Damage, reach, timing windows, the level, the gear list, the saves and the controls. A roll still cuts any strike short, as before.

## Full test checklist

1. Sound in the browser: music on the title card, in the hollow and at the cart. Effects for jump, land, swing, hit, coin, pickup and the door.
2. Sound on Android: the same list.
3. Settings: Music and Sound each step from Off to Full and you hear the change at once.
4. Each weapon in the short list: rusty sword, dusk blade, twig knife, mallet and hunting bow. Does each feel less stiff? Say which still feels wrong.
5. The weapon stays in Wren's hand through every strike, facing left and right.
6. The streak follows the blade and fades fast. It does not linger or flash.
7. The hit pause is a blink, not a stutter. Tell me if it feels too long.
8. A roll during a strike still works at once.
9. Abilities: throwing knives, pinecone bomb, bark shield and warm tea each show a motion.
10. Music keeps looping after a few minutes in one place.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried.
- Nobody listened to the sound. The test measures that sound comes out of the page, not how it sounds. I chose the three tracks by their tags (dark, mysterious, scary), their maker and a measure of how busy and bright each one is. Tell me the mood of any that sounds wrong and I will swap it.
- Sound on Android was not tried. The fix is a standard Godot audio file and it applies there too.
- How the strikes feel at full speed. The test browser runs slowly. The tests confirm every frame plays, the streak draws, the pause ends and the weapon data is right. The feel is yours to judge.
- The browser tests ran with a 640 x 360 page, as in v0.2.0.
