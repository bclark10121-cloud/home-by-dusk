# Home by Dusk v0.5.0

The whip in hand. Between strikes Wren now carries her whip: a small coil in her hand with a short tail that sways, streams behind her when she runs and flies up when she jumps. The whip has a painted braided leather look and a wrapped grip with brass bands. A hit with the tip scores 6 in place of 4. The whip stops at rock and no longer passes through walls. A button press is remembered for a tenth of a second, so a press at the end of a roll still counts.

This is stage 2 of the motion plan you approved on 2026-11-15.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK over the last one. The title card shows Version 0.5.0.
2. Start or continue. Stand still and look at Wren's hand. The Leather Whip hangs there as a small coil with a short tail. It sways a little in the draft.
3. Run right across the open ground. The tail streams behind her and the coil swings with her arm.
4. Jump and double jump. The tail flies up as she falls and drapes on the ground when she lands.
5. Walk to the training dummy and stop about four steps short of it, at the far end of the whip's reach. Press the first weapon button once (J, X or the touch button). The tip lands: a bright ring at the dummy and a 6. If you see a 4, step back a little and try again.
6. Step in until Wren almost touches the dummy. Press again. A 4 shows. The near part of the whip still hits, without the bonus.
7. Look closely at the whip during a crack: braided leather and a wrapped grip with brass bands.
8. Press the second weapon button for the sword combo. The whip moves to her belt while she holds the sword, then back to her hand.
9. Climb the stone steps past the dummy to the foot of the tall rock wall. Face the wall and crack the whip. It stops at the rock with a little puff of dust.
10. Roll (L, B or the touch button) and press the first weapon button just before the roll ends. The crack comes out as soon as the roll is over.

## New in this version

- The whip in hand. A coil in her hand and a short tail, moved by simple rope physics on every tick. The coil swings under her hand like a pendulum. Both follow her run, her jumps, her turns and her lean.
- The whip on her belt. While she holds the sword, the bow or another weapon, or throws or casts an ability, the whip hangs from her belt.
- A tip bonus. The last quarter of the whip's reach, about one tile, is the tip. A hit there deals one and a half times the damage: 6 in place of 4 for the Leather Whip. It shows a bright ring at the hit and pushes the foe a little more. You chose this number on 2026-11-16.
- Walls. The whip stops where it meets rock, and so does its hit box. A foe behind a wall is safe from it.
- Button memory. A press up to 0.1 s before Wren is free to act still counts: at the end of a roll, a landing or a ledge climb. Abilities get the same memory.
- A painted whip. Gemini painted a braided lash and a wrapped grip in the game's style. The other whips (Vine and Spark, not handed out yet) tint the same leather to their own colour.
- Wren's hand spot is now known on every frame of every move, not only during strikes. The whip uses it to hang from the right place.

## Changed

- During a crack the whip now leaves the hand you see, with her lean and squash. Before, it started from the hand without the lean.
- A new headless test, `--whip`, checks the tip bonus, the near hit, the wall, button memory and that the whip in hand never sinks into rock.

## Not changed

- Jump, double jump, run speed and roll distance. The feel numbers are the same as in v0.4.0.
- The whip's timing, reach and normal damage. The sword and every other weapon.
- The level, the gear list, saves and settings. A trip saved in v0.4.0 continues in v0.5.0.

## Judgment calls

- The tip zone is the last quarter of the whip's reach (about one tile).
- The whip in hand is a coil plus a 14 unit tail, not the whole 64 unit whip. The whole whip would drag far behind her.
- The whip hangs from her belt while another weapon or an ability is in her hand.
- The tip hit also pushes 30 percent harder.
- Button memory covers both weapon buttons and both ability buttons.
- No Meshy work this release.
