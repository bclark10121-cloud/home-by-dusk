# Home by Dusk v0.2.0

The painted storybook look. v0.1.0's pixel art read as too low in resolution, so the whole game is redrawn in HD: painted scenery, props, foes and gear, a misty dusk forest in layers and Wren rendered at four times the detail. The level, the moves and the rules are the same, so this build is for judging the look and the feel together.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page and press Play in your browser, or install the APK over v0.1.0. The title card shows Home by Dusk and Version 0.2.0, with the painted hollow behind it. Press Start or Continue.
2. Stand still for a moment. Look for: the lit cottage window, smoke from the chimney, the torch glow that flickers, fireflies, fog between three rows of trees and the stars in the dusk sky.
3. Walk right. The trees and ruins behind the path slide at different speeds. Dark ferns pass close to the eye at the bottom of the screen. Wren has a dark ink outline and a soft shadow on the ground.
4. Swing at the training dummy with J (keyboard), X (Xbox) or the first weapon button (touch). The sword in her hand is the painted rusty sword. The damage numbers are sharp.
5. Jump the grassy steps and drop into the hollow pit. The ground is painted stone and earth with a grass edge on every open top.
6. Meet a mossling, a puffcap and a moth. Each is a painted creature now. They still warn before they strike. Roll through a lunge.
7. Climb the tall wall for the throwing knives and cross the plank ledges for the hunting bow. Check the pictures in the gear slots at the bottom left.
8. Break the cracked wall block with three hits. It darkens with each hit, then opens the hidden room with the flower and the chest.
9. Talk to Pip at the cart (E, RB or Use). The cart sells from a short list this release: 4 weapons and 4 abilities. Buy something or a cup of tea.
10. Go through the glowing stone door for run 2. Then open the Menu and try Journal, Settings and Head home.

## New in this version

- A new look: painted storybook art in HD. Four times the detail of v0.1.0 on every sprite.
- Scenery and props painted with Gemini and cut into game art: the cottage, Pip and the cart, the stone door, the dummy, torches, signs, chests, the cracked wall, planks, pines, a gnarled tree, arches, a pillar, ferns, bushes, mushrooms, rocks and grass.
- Foes repainted: mossling, puffcap and moth.
- All 20 weapons and 20 abilities repainted, with the six keepsakes, the coin and the projectiles.
- Wren: the same Meshy model, shown at 192 pixel frames with an ink outline in place of the 48 pixel sheet. The moves and timings did not change.
- Painted ground: a stone and earth texture, shade that deepens away from the air, ink lines on cliff sides and a grass strip on every open top.
- A dusk forest in depth: a sky with stars, three misty rows of trees and ruins, drifting fog, trees right behind the path, plants in front of Wren's feet and dark plants close to the eye.
- Light and air: warm glows on the window, torches, cart lanterns and door, torch flicker, fireflies, chimney smoke, soft ground shadows and darker corners.
- A new painted app icon.
- A short gear list for now: drops, chests and Pip's cart hand out 4 weapons (dusk blade, twig knife, mallet, hunting bow) and 4 abilities (throwing knives, pinecone bomb, bark shield, warm tea), plus the rusty sword. The other 31 items wait in the data and in Tester tools.

## Changed

- The smooth camera no longer snaps to whole pixels. That was for pixel art.
- The title card is a little clearer so the painted hollow shows behind it.
- The journal counts weapons and abilities against this release's short list.

## Not changed

- The level layout, the moves, the foes' behaviour, the numbers, the saves and the controls. A v0.1.0 save opens as it was, gear included.

## Full test checklist

1. Title card: Start or Continue, Play and Settings work with touch, keyboard and an Xbox controller.
2. Every sprite is sharp at your screen size: Wren, foes, props, gear pictures, coins and keepsakes. Nothing is blocky and nothing has a pink edge.
3. The game runs smoothly on your phone while walking through the whole hollow. Tell me where it slows down.
4. Movement feels the same as v0.1.0: jump, double jump, roll, ledge climb, drop through a plank ledge.
5. Wren's feet sit on the grass line. Foes, chests, signs and torches sit on the ground and do not float.
6. The held weapon lines up with her hand through each swing, thrust, slam, shot and throw.
7. A foe hit costs energy. At zero energy Wren heads home. Coins and keepsakes stay.
8. Pip's cart only offers the short list. Tester tools, Gear, still offers all 20 weapons and 20 abilities.
9. The cracked wall breaks in three hits and comes back on the next run.
10. Camera Near, Normal and Far all look right. The backdrop never shows a gap or a hard edge.
11. A v0.1.0 save still opens with its coins, keepsakes and gear.
12. Touch: walk, jump, roll, both weapons, both abilities and Use.

## Not tested

- Real devices: no phone, tablet, Retroid or real Xbox controller was tried. Speed on a real phone is the biggest open question for the HD art. The test browser draws without a graphics chip, so its speed says little about a phone.
- The browser test ran with a 640 x 360 page. At 1280 x 720 the test browser reached only 3 to 4 frames a second, too slow for timed button presses. The layout is the same at both sizes.
- Sound was not listened to. No sound changed.
- Touch controls were not tried on a small phone.
