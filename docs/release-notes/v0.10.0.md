# Home by Dusk v0.10.0

Home by Dusk moves to Godot 4.7.2, draws at your screen's own rate (up to 120 frames a second on your phone), opens full screen in the browser on the first tap and fills wide screens edge to edge. Your whip, run and weapon notes from the playtest sweep come in their own release once you say what felt off.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.9.1. Your saves stay.
2. Run, jump and crack the whip. Motion feels the same, smoother on a 120 Hz screen.
3. Settings > Tester tools > Show frame rate (or Settings > Frame rate). It reads up to 120 while you run.
4. In a browser, the first tap turns full screen on. Turning Full screen off in Settings is remembered.

## New in this version

- Smooth at up to 120 frames a second (G6.20): no 60 cap. The game already smooths Wren's motion between physics steps. A new frame check proves she moves on every frame at 60, 90 and 120.
- Show frame rate in Tester tools.
- Full screen by default in the browser (G5.11, kit 1.17).
- 1080p and wider screens fill edge to edge (G6.21).

## Fixed

- The whip's grip could sit inside the floor for one step while Wren rolled with the whip hanging. The grip now sits on the floor's surface. Found by the whip check on Godot 4.7.

## Changed

- Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19). Kit 1.18.0.

## Install

Version 0.10.0 installs over 0.9.1 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Running and jumping feel the same as before, smoother on your phone.
- [ ] The frame rate reads up to 120 on your phone. Battery saver caps at 30.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine with Godot 4.7.2:

- ROUTE ok (0 resets), WHIP ok (4 of 4 runs), MOVES ok, the reach check, the feel numbers (jump 50 px, double jump 85 px, run 140 px a second, roll 62 px), COMMON, SECRETS and SAVESTORE.
- New frame check (`--fixed-fps <hz> -- --frames --hz=<hz>`): 140 px a second at 60, 90 and 120, no stalls, no pace jumps.
- Browser: the standards test with the pretend Xbox controller prints `errors []`, the audio test `AUDIO ok`, the wide check at 1920 x 1080 and 2400 x 1080 `WIDE check ok`.
- The build check: both builds carry the Tester tools gate and the APK asks for no internet.

Not tested: a real phone, the Retroid or an Xbox controller on a device.
